One

Symptom check

Check all that apply.

  • You are warm in a room where nobody else is warm.
  • You have blamed the room.
  • You removed one article of clothing and described it as getting comfortable.
  • Someone asked if you were okay and you said "yeah, it's just hot in here."
  • You have stopped talking, but you are still nodding.
  • You are reading this at a barbecue.

One or more: you have the pork sweats.

Zero: you have not eaten enough pork. This is a separate problem and it is worse.

Three

The four stages

  • Confidence

    "I could eat this every day," you say. Out loud. To people who will remember.

  • The sheen

    You blame the room. You are the only one blaming the room.

  • The unbuttoning

    Framed as comfort. It is not comfort. Everyone at the table understands what they have just witnessed.

  • Ambient

    You are now a heat source. Small children give you space. A thermostat somewhere is making a decision it will not be able to justify.

Four

The science

Digesting protein costs 20–30% of its own calories. Carbohydrates cost 5–15%. Fat costs 3%. Eat enough pork and the arithmetic says you should warm up.

That is the entire theory. It is not a medical term. No study confirms it. Merriam-Webster has declined to comment, which we are choosing to read as fear.

Evidence: everyone who has had it.

Five

Management

Prevention

Three to six ounces. Eat a vegetable. Drink water during rather than after.

No one has ever done this.

Treatment

Asked how to handle the meat sweats, a charcuterie expert told Food & Wine that you basically have to pass out and sweat it out.

That is the state of the art. That is the whole field.

Important safety information

Pork Sweats is not a drug, device, treatment, or product. Pork Sweats is a thing that happens to you. Side effects may include a second plate, a nap you did not schedule, and telling the story at work on Monday.

Do not operate a smoker while experiencing stage IV. Do not attempt to explain the condition to someone currently experiencing it. Ask your friends whether barbecue is right for you; they will say yes, because they also want barbecue.
